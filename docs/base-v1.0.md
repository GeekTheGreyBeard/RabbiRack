## Pieces and dimensions

Print one `base-fitment.stl`, one `rear-foot.stl` and two `attachment-key-0p25.stl` from `models/base-v1.0/` for a new base assembly. Do not duplicate the keys if they are already installed.

- Deck: 106 × 110 × 14 mm; total saddle height 37 mm.
- Backward tilt: 12.5°; repeating width: 106 mm.
- Removable rear foot: 54 × 48 × 14 mm body, with integrated tongue; assembled depth extends to 158.3 mm.
- Authentic traced Rabbit emblem: 32 mm high, recessed 3.175 mm; 10.825 mm floor remains below it.
- Rack interface: bottom bowtie centers X22 and X84, using the accepted V2 socket shape and 0.25 mm inset keys.

Seat the empty rack along its tilted vertical axis, then insert the two attachment keys from the front. Lower the rear foot into its open-top dovetail socket. It is intentionally removable upward, not a lifting handle. Do not force tight mating parts.

Two top-access side pockets per edge, at Y25 and Y85, permit future sideways base expansion. They need no extra joining keys for a single base. Side trim covers these otherwise unused pockets. Install base side trim before rack side trim.

## Validation and print orientation

The original concept saddles were replaced with united, bed-supported keyed saddles. The initially detected underside overhang was corrected by pedestals reaching the deck. Final slicing required no supports. The foot is exported inverted so its full tongue silhouette begins on the bed; keys print flat.

Digital checks found closed manifold solids, no nominal assembled intersections, clear sampled insertion paths, and positive key engagement against uplift. A nominal single Rabbit body's vertical projection lies inside the base footprint. This is not a pull-force, impact, phone-load or multi-height stability certification. Check seating, wobble and footing for the actual arrangement.
