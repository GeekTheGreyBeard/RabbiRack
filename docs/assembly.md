# Assembly

1. Cool parts and remove strings carefully without sanding away mating faces. Check each keyed fit gently before loading a device.
2. Lower the rear foot's dovetail tongue into the base's open-top rear socket. The foot is removable vertically and is not a lifting handle.
3. Seat the rack's lower rim in the inclined saddles; insert two bowties from the front into the matching base/rack sockets. The base gives 12.5° backward tilt.
4. For another row or column, align cell edges on the 106 mm pitch and insert two keys per seam. Join adjacent base decks with two keys if coupling columns. Support the assembly while inserting keys; do not force it.
5. Seat two lower caps with lips inward. With the frame supported horizontally and top keeper removed, place the r1 against the rear support and slide it approximately 3 mm under the lower lips. Insert the keeper gently. Reverse for removal.
6. Check each device's retention, rocking, camera/control access and charging connector clearance. Return the populated rack to a flat surface; check foot seating and stability before leaving it loaded.

Route cables through the back and provide slack so cable tension does not pull on the rack or USB connector. Owner-added cable attachment hardware is not specified or supplied. The published core leaves rear openings accessible; the closed-trim work in progress intentionally has no exterior cable notches.

Optional historical trim must be installed on exposed edges only. Fit base side trims before rack side rails; remove in reverse order. Top caps cover the side-rail ends at exposed upper edges. Trim-fit confirmation is pending separately from the accepted base fit.

Printed friction-fit retainers are not transport restraints. The photographed populated build and owner fit acceptance do not certify impact resistance, arbitrary stack heights or a universal load rating. The historical phone panel does not hold its intended phone securely and should not be used.
