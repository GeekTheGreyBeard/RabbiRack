# Acceptance and provenance

The preserved rack cell and retainers were owner accepted in October 2026. Base v1.0 was subsequently printed and the owner reported perfect fitment. Original mesh bytes remain unchanged. The repeated red base job also completed successfully. These are owner fit/print outcomes, not load certification.

On October 25 the owner supplied three photographs of four r1 devices in a two-column, two-high assembly and authorized their public release. Their original filenames are retained for publication copies. EXIF orientation was applied before stripping metadata; opaque redactions and tight framing remove unrelated monitor conversations and applications. Private originals remain private. Photos show a physical assembly, while the existing CAD illustrations remain illustrations.

Notched trim v1.0 completed its machine job; a separate physical-fit claim is not inferred. Closed trim v1.1 was still in the active red batch during release preparation and is not distributed as stable geometry. The phone panel's overall envelope is compatible, but the owner reports retainers too open for secure retention. That known defect remains unresolved and the phone files are reference-only.

The project was prepared with AI assistance. Existing source helpers were recovered/inlined and prior regeneration verified with Blender 5.0.1. The v1 release preserves geometry and source files. Binary STL checksums are recorded in models/SHA256SUMS. Build scripts report mesh connectivity, manifold edges and volume; nominal assembly checks cannot prove printed strength, cable fit or retention.

The owner authorized CC0 for original contributions. Vendor Rabbit emblem trace remains separately identified and excluded; see attribution.md. Printer-specific files, access information, private photos and execution logs are excluded from publication.
